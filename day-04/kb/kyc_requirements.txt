NovaTrust Bank follows strict Know Your Customer (KYC) guidelines for account opening and periodic profile updates.
To complete KYC verification, customers must submit one synthetic Identity Proof and one synthetic Address Proof.
Accepted Identity Proof documents: NovaTrust Universal ID (NUID), Fictional Citizen Card (FCC), or Synthetic Passport.
Accepted Address Proof documents: Utility Bill (less than 3 months old), Lease Certificate, or Official Citizen Address Sheet.
In-person verification can be completed at any branch, or digitally via NovaTrust Video-KYC on the official mobile app.
Video-KYC is available from Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and requires a physical display of original synthetic documents.
Periodic KYC updates are required every 2 years for high-risk accounts and every 5 years for standard savings accounts.
If KYC documentation remains incomplete after notice expiry, account operations will be temporarily suspended after 30 days.
Suspended accounts cannot perform debit transactions, ATM withdrawals, or UPI transfers until fresh KYC documents are verified.
