NovaTrust Bank offers collateral-free Personal Loans for personal, medical, educational, or emergency financial needs.
Applicants must be between 21 and 58 years of age at the time of loan application submission.
Minimum monthly net income requirement is 25,000 NovaCredits for salaried individuals and 35,000 NovaCredits for self-employed professionals.
Salaried applicants must have at least 1 year of total continuous work experience with 6 months at their current employer.
Self-employed applicants must present registered business continuity proof for at least 2 consecutive financial years.
Required documents: Last 3 months' pay slips, 6 months' NovaTrust bank statement, synthetic KYC documents, and income declaration.
Personal loan amounts range from a minimum of 50,000 NovaCredits to a maximum limit of 1,000,000 NovaCredits.
Loan repayment tenures are flexible, ranging from 12 months (1 year) to 60 months (5 years) with fixed monthly EMIs.
Interest rates for personal loans start at 10.5% per annum, depending on credit score and monthly income profile.
