NovaTrust Bank offers three primary credit card variants tailored to different spending habits: Silver, Gold, and Platinum.
The NovaTrust Silver Card has an annual fee of 500 NovaCredits, waived if annual spending exceeds 25,000 NovaCredits.
Silver Card eligibility requires a minimum age of 21, a monthly income of 15,000 NovaCredits, and basic KYC approval.
The NovaTrust Gold Card has an annual fee of 1,500 NovaCredits and offers 2x reward points on all online purchases.
Gold Card eligibility requires a minimum age of 21, a monthly income of 45,000 NovaCredits, and an active NovaTrust savings account.
The NovaTrust Platinum Card has an annual fee of 4,000 NovaCredits and features unlimited lounge access and 5x rewards.
Platinum Card eligibility requires a minimum age of 25 and a monthly income of 100,000 NovaCredits.
All cardholders get a 45-day interest-free grace period on purchases and a daily ATM cash advance limit of 10,000 NovaCredits.
Card statement generation occurs on the 1st of every month, with the payment due date fixed for the 20th of the same month.
