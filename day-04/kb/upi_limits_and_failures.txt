NovaTrust Bank UPI service allows instant peer-to-peer and peer-to-merchant fund transfers directly from your savings account.
The daily total UPI transaction limit per user is set at 100,000 NovaCredits across all connected UPI applications.
The maximum per-transaction limit for a single UPI transfer is 25,000 NovaCredits.
Users are allowed a maximum of 10 UPI transactions per calendar day per account.
Common failure scenarios include incorrect UPI PIN, insufficient account balance, bank server downtime, or exceeding daily limits.
If money is debited from your account during a failed transaction, NovaTrust initiates an auto-reversal within 24 hours.
If the funds are not credited back within 24 hours, customers should log a ticket using the 12-digit UPI Transaction Reference Number.
Unresolved UPI failure complaints are resolved within a guaranteed timeline of 3 business days by NovaTrust support.
Never share your 6-digit UPI PIN or OTP with anyone; NovaTrust staff will never request your PIN.
