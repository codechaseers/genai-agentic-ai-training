NovaTrust Bank offers Fixed Deposit (FD) schemes to help customers earn guaranteed interest on their savings.
The minimum deposit amount required to open a NovaTrust Fixed Deposit is 5,000 NovaCredits, with no maximum cap.
FD tenures range from a minimum of 7 days to a maximum of 10 years (120 months).
Interest rates range from 5.0% per annum for short tenures up to 7.5% per annum for tenures of 1 to 3 years.
Senior citizens (aged 60 and above) receive an additional interest rate top-up of 0.50% per annum across all tenures.
Interest can be paid out monthly, quarterly, or accumulated till maturity under the reinvestment (cumulative) option.
Premature withdrawal of FD is permitted, subject to a premature closure penalty fee of 1.0% on the applicable interest rate.
Partial withdrawal is available in multiples of 1,000 NovaCredits without closing the entire Fixed Deposit account.
Upon maturity, FDs automatically renew for the same tenure at prevailing interest rates unless auto-closure is pre-selected.
