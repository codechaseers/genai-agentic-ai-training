NovaTrust Bank offers a standard Savings Account designed for everyday banking needs.
To maintain an active savings account, customers must keep a minimum monthly average balance of 5,000 NovaCredits.
Accounts can be opened online via the NovaTrust Mobile App or in person at any branch using synthetic KYC verification.
Savings accounts earn an annual interest rate of 4.5%, calculated daily and credited to the account quarterly.
Cash deposits up to 50,000 NovaCredits per month are free of charge at all NovaTrust branches and automated deposit kiosks.
Account holders receive 5 free ATM cash withdrawals per month at NovaTrust ATMs; subsequent withdrawals incur a 20 NovaCredit fee.
Daily debit card withdrawal limit for standard savings account holders is capped at 25,000 NovaCredits.
Customers can check their balance 24/7 through net banking, the mobile app, or by sending SMS 'BAL' to 55000.
If the account balance falls below the required 5,000 NovaCredits, a non-maintenance charge of 150 NovaCredits per month is applied.
